break in policy.

Sum insured , the waiting periods if any shall start afresh only for the enhance
portion of the sum insured.
Moratorium period: After the expiry of Moratorium Period no health insurance
policy shall be contestable except for proven fraud and permanent exclusions
specified in the policy contract.
