PROSPECTUS
This prospectus is designed for our end customers who plan to or have already bought ` Chola Protracted Package
policy for Two Wheelers’ from Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company for their Two Wheelers used for
social, domestic or pleasure purposes. Details about policy coverages, exclusions are part of this document.
Why do I need to insure my Vehicle?
Insuring your vehicle protects you from a financial loss arising out of loss or damage to your vehicle, your liability
towards third parties for personal injury, death and property damage on account of any accident involving your
vehicle. Furthermore you are also responsible for the safety of yourself.
Under the provisions of Motor Vehicles Act, all the vehicles that ply in public places must have an insurance policy
that at least covers ‘Third Party Liability’ as specified under the MV Act.
1. Who is providing coverage under the Policy?
Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company Ltd., a joint venture between Murugappa Group & Mitsui
Sumitomo Insurance Group, Japan is providing the coverage under the Policy.
2. Who is eligible for the coverage?
Any resident of India who owns a Two Wheeler as defined in Central Motor Vehicles Act is eligible for the
coverage. Any Vehicle that is owned by a company, a partnership firm or a similar body corporate is also eligible
for the coverage.
3. What is the coverage under Chola long term Private Car package policy?
This is a comprehensive policy which covers both ‘Third Party liability (Act Only Cover)’ and ‘Own Damage Cover’.

Section II - Third Party (TP) Liability:
➢ Personal Liability: As per the latest provisions of Motor Vehicles Act.
➢ Property Liability: In the event of your vehicle causing ‘Third Party’ property damage, Chola MS will offer
compensation up to Rs 7, 50, 000.
Section III - Compulsory Personal Accident (CPA) cover for Owner-Driver: –
The CPA cover offers compensation upto Rs.15 lakhs for personal injuries suffered by the registered Owner of the
Insured Vehicle whilst driving, travelling or alighting into/out of the Insured Vehicle. The registered Owner should
hold an effective driving license. The Compensation will be paid to the nominee in an unfortunate event of death
of the registered vehicle owner.
