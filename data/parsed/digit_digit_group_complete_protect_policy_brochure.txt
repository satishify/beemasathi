5. POLICY COVERAGE (Refer “C. Benefit Covered under the Policy” of Policy Wordings)
I. Coverage
SECTION 1. ACCIDENTAL DEATH (Benefit Cover): If you suffer an accidental injury that causes death within 12
months, 100% of the insured sum will be paid to your nominee as per the policy. Additional inbuilt benefits
include:
• Disappearance: If the body isn't found within 12 months after incidents like a crash or disaster and
presumed dead, the full sum is paid.
• Drowning: If presumed dead due to drowning and the body isn't found within 12 months, the full sum is
paid.
For both, the nominee/legal heir must provide an indemnity bond to repay the amount if the insured is later
found alive. After the claim is paid, the policy and related benefits for that person end.
<<SECTION 2. PERMANENT TOTAL DISABLEMENT(Benefit Cover):>> If this Cover has been opted and You
sustain an Accidental Bodily Injury during the Policy Period, which is the sole and direct cause of Your
“Permanent Total Disablement” within twelve (12) months from the Date of accident, then We will pay 100%
of Sum Insured, as opted by You and mentioned in Your Policy Schedule/Certificate of Insurance against this
Section.
<<SECTION 3. PERMANENT PARTIAL DISABLEMENT(Benefit Cover):>> If this Cover has been opted and You
sustain an Accidental Bodily Injury during the Policy Period, which is the sole and direct cause of Your
Permanent Partial Disablement within twelve (12) months from the Date of accident, then We will pay the
percentage of Sum Insured, as opted by You and mentioned in Your Policy Schedule/Certificate of Insurance
against this Section, as per the Scale mentioned in the policy wordings.
<<SECTION 4. LOSS OF INCOME BENEFIT(Benefit Cover):>> If this Cover has been opted and You sustain an
Accidental Bodily Injury during the Policy Period, which is the sole and direct cause of a Temporary Total
Disablement and which completely prevents You from performing each and every duty pertaining to Your
employment or occupation on a temporary basis, then We will pay a weekly benefit, amount of which is
mentioned in Your Policy Schedule/Certificate of Insurance against this Section, provided that conditions are
met as mentioned in the policy wordings.
<<SECTION 5. CHILDREN EDUCATION BENEFIT(Benefit Cover):>> If a claim is approved under "Accidental
Death" or "Permanent Total Disablement," the policy covers your dependent child(ren)'s education costs, up
to the Sum Insured, even if they’re not insured under the policy. Eligible children must be unmarried, under
25, and full-time students. For minors, the claim is payable to legal heirs, subject to policy terms and
conditions.
<<SECTION 6. MARRIAGE EXPENSE FOR CHILDREN BENEFIT(Benefit Cover):>> If a claim is approved under
"Accidental Death" or "Permanent Total Disablement," the policy will cover the marriage expenses of your
dependent child(ren) up to the Sum Insured. Eligible children must be under 25 years, unmarried, and the
maximum payout remains the Sum Insured, regardless of the number of children. For minors, the claim will
be payable to legal heirs.

conditions, limitations and exclusions mentioned in the Policy.

to policy terms conditions, limitations and exclusions mentioned in the Policy.

<<SECTION 24. EMI PROTECTION COVER (Indemnity Cover) :>> This cover ensures payment of your loan EMIs

reached. Proof of the condition and compliance with terms (e.g., no overdue EMIs, survival period of 30 days
for critical illness) are required. Pre-existing conditions and diagnoses within the waiting period are excluded.
Permanent partial disablement includes loss of limbs, sight, or specific body parts, as defined.
<<SECTION 25. LOSS OF EMPLOYMENT (Indemnity Cover):>> If you opt for this cover and are terminated,
dismissed, or retrenched during the policy period as per employer rules or legal directives, you are eligible
for claims based on one of two methods chosen at policy inception. The options include either a percentage
of take-home salary (70%) or a fixed/EMI-based payout, as detailed in the policy. Claims have specific
restrictions, such as fraud, poor performance, voluntary unemployment, probationary or temporary roles, or
employment contracts outside India. Waiting periods and re-employment terms further affect claim eligibility
and payouts. Benefits commence after severance periods and additional waiting periods, with provisions for
reduced claims in case of re-employment.

f. Check the updated list of Network Providers before availing Cashless Facility.

B. Reimbursement Claim Process:
Reimbursement Facility is available at any hospital in India. You must pay the hospital directly and submit
documents to the Service Provider/TPA for claim reimbursement
1. We or Our Service Provider / Third Party Administrator (TPA) should be intimated within 48 hours of
date of admission.
2. For Reimbursement Claim You shall follow the below Procedure:
a. The Company shall settle or reject a claim, as the case may be, within 15 days from submission of
Claim.
b. In case the claim is not settled within the specified timelines, then the claimant is entitled for
interest at bank rate plus 2 percent from the date of receipt of intimation to till the date of
payment.
“Bank rate” shall mean the rate fixed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) at the beginning of the
financial year in which claim has fallen due.
c. In case of Your Death, We shall reimburse the claim amount to Your Nominee as named in Your
