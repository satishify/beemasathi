5. POLICY COVERAGE (Refer “C. Benefit Covered under the Policy” of Policy Wordings)
<<SECTION 1. ACCIDENTAL DEATH: (Benefit cover)>> If you sustain an accidental injury that leads to death within
12 months, the policy pays 100% of the insured amount. This includes cases of disappearance or drowning,
provided certain conditions are met, like an indemnity bond from the nominee. Once a claim is accepted,
additional benefits under the policy for that person will cease.
<<SECTION 2. PERMANENT TOTAL DISABLEMENT: (Benefit cover)>> If you suffer accidental injury leading to
permanent total disablement within 12 months, the policy pays 100% of the insured sum. For multiple
disablements, liability is limited to the insured sum, and additional benefits for that person cease once a claim is
accepted. This cover is subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions in the Policy.
<<SECTION 3. PERMANENT PARTIAL DISABLEMENT: (Benefit cover)>> If you sustain accidental injury causing
permanent partial disablement within 12 months, the policy pays a percentage of the insured sum based on the
injury (as per a defined scale mentioned in the policy wordings). If multiple disablements occur, liability is capped
at the total insured sum, and unlisted disablements are assessed by an external medical advisor. Coverage under
this benefit continues after a claim, subject to policy terms and the remaining insured amount.
<<SECTION 4. LOSS OF INCOME BENEFIT: (Benefit cover)>> If accidental injury leads to temporary total
disablement, the policy provides a weekly benefit as per the Policy Schedule. Payments stop when you can resume
work or after the maximum eligible weeks, and the benefit is capped at your net weekly income. Certification by a
Medical Practitioner and adherence to policy terms, including time limits, are required for claims.
<<SECTION 5. CHILDREN EDUCATION BENEFIT: (Benefit cover)>> If a claim for accidental death or permanent total
disablement is accepted, the policy covers the dependent child's education costs (up to the sum insured) if they
are under 25, unmarried, and a full-time student. For minors, the payout will be made to legal heirs, subject to
policy terms and conditions.
<<SECTION 6. MARRIAGE EXPENSE FOR CHILDREN BENEFIT: (Benefit cover)>> If a claim for accidental death or
permanent total disablement is accepted, the policy provides the insured sum for the marriage expenses of
dependent children under 25 years, unmarried at the time of the accident. For minors, the payout goes to legal
heirs, subject to policy terms and conditions.
<<SECTION 7. ORPHAN BENEFIT FOR CHILDREN: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover and a claim for
accidental death is accepted & both parents die in accidents during the policy period, we will pay the sum insured
to your dependent child, provided they are under 25, unmarried, and have no independent income. Payments for
minors go to legal guardians or heirs.
<<SECTION 8. FUNERAL EXPENSES: (Benefit cover)>> If a claim for accidental death is accepted, the policy covers
funeral, cremation, or burial expenses of the insured person, up to the sum insured.
<<SECTION 9. TRANSPORTATION EXPENSE: (Benefit cover)>> If a claim for accidental death is accepted, the policy
covers transportation expenses for the insured person's mortal remains to a burial or cremation ground, or their
residence, up to the insured sum.

disablement, or permanent partial disablement is accepted, the policy reimburses professional counselling
expenses (up to the insured sum) within six months of the incident. Coverage applies to the insured person's family
in case of death, and to the insured themselves in case of disablement.

A. Hospitalization Expenses: If you have this cover and are hospitalized due to an accidental injury, we will cover

C. Pre-Hospitalization Expenses: Covers expenses incurred <<30/60/90>> days before hospitalization, as per

D. Post-Hospitalization Expenses: Covers expenses incurred <<60/90/120/180>> days post hospitalization, as per

E. Dental Treatment: Covers necessary dental treatment expenses after an accident.
F. Road Ambulance: Covers emergency ambulance transportation to the hospital
G. Second Medical Opinion: Will arrange and cover the cost for a second opinion from our panel of doctors if you
have a major accidental injury requiring hospitalization.
H. Transportation of Imported Medicine: Reimburse freight charges for importing necessary medicines not
available in India
<<SECTION 12. HOME (DOMICILIARY) HOSPITALIZATION: (Indemnity cover)>> If you opt for this cover, the policy
reimburses medical expenses for accidental injury treatment at home, provided hospitalization was necessary but
not possible. Coverage applies if treatment lasts at least 3 days, and excludes non-accidental conditions, subject
to policy terms and conditions.
<<SECTION 13. LONG HOSPITALIZATION CASH BENEFIT: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover and experience
accidental injury requiring hospitalization for the minimum consecutive days mentioned in the Policy Schedule,
the policy pays a lump sum amount. This benefit is payable once per person during the policy period, subject to
terms and conditions.
<<SECTION 14. DAILY HOSPITAL CASH COVER: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover, the policy provides a daily
cash allowance for each completed 24 hours of hospitalization due to accidental injury, for the maximum days
specified in the Policy Schedule. For ICU stays, the allowance is twice the Daily Cash Allowance.
<<SECTION 15. OUT-PATIENT (OPD) BENEFIT: (Indemnity cover)>> If you opt for this cover, the policy reimburses
reasonable charges for out-patient treatments due to accidental injuries, including doctor consultations, diagnostic
tests, minor surgeries, prescribed medicines, dental treatments, and rehabilitation like physiotherapy or
counselling. However, it excludes expenses for items like hearing aids, spectacles, cosmetic procedures, and non-
accident-related vaccinations, as per policy terms.
<<SECTION 16. EMERGENCY AIR AMBULANCE: (Indemnity cover)>> If you opt for this cover, the policy reimburses
expenses for emergency air or helicopter transportation to the nearest hospital for life-threatening conditions after
an accident, provided it is medically necessary and approved by a medical practitioner. This benefit applies only if
a claim under Section 11 (Accidental Hospitalization Cover) has been accepted, subject to policy terms.
<<SECTION 17. COMA BENEFIT COVER: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover and an accidental injury leads to
a coma requiring ICU hospitalization within 30 days, the policy pays the insured sum. The coma must meet specific
medical criteria, with permanent neurological deficits confirmed after 30 days, and excludes cases caused by
alcohol, drugs, or non-accidental illness, subject to policy terms and conditions.
<<SECTION 18. FRACTURE COVER: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover and sustain accidental fractures, the
policy pays a percentage of the insured sum based on the nature and severity of the fractures (as detailed in the
table mentioned in policy wordings). Payments are determined by medical criteria and capped at the insured sum,
excluding fractures caused by illnesses like osteoporosis or malignancy, subject to policy terms.
<<SECTION 19. BURNS COVER: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover, the policy pays a percentage of the
insured sum for second- or third-degree burns caused by an accident, based on the affected area and severity (as
per the table mentioned in the policy wordings). Coverage excludes self-inflicted burns and requires medical
confirmation, with liability capped at the insured sum, subject to policy terms and conditions.

<<SECTION 22. COMPASSIONATE VISIT: (Indemnity cover)>> If a claim for accidental death, permanent total

female insured persons, subject to policy terms.
<<SECTION 24. ADVENTURE SPORTS COVER: (Both Indemnity and Assured Benefit Basis)>> If you opt for this
cover, the policy provides benefits for accidental injury in Adventure Sports (List of Adventure Sports Activities
Covered is mentioned in the policy wordings) covering death, permanent total disablement (100% of the insured
sum), or hospitalization (expenses up to the insured sum). The activities must be non-professional and supervised
by a trained professional, and coverage is subject to specific options selected in your Policy Schedule and terms
of the Policy.
<<SECTION 25. CRITICAL ILLNESS: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover, the policy pays the insured sum for
critical illnesses or covered surgical procedures specified in the plan, provided it occurs for the first time, and
after the waiting period. Claims are subject to survival for 30 days after diagnosis (unless waived), and pre-existing
conditions are excluded unless disclosed and accepted, with coverage ceasing after payment. (Plan wise Covered
Critical Illnesses is mentioned in the policy wordings)
<<SECTION 26. HIV COVER: (Benefit cover)>> If you opt for this cover and are first diagnosed with an HIV infection
during the policy period (excluding transmission via unprotected sex), the policy pays the insured sum. Coverage
terminates for the diagnosed person after a claim but continues for other sections of the policy, subject to terms,
conditions, and waiting periods.
<<SECTION 27. EMI PROTECTION COVER: (Indemnity cover)>> If you opt for this cover, the policy pays your EMI
contribution for a loan from a financial institution if accidental injury, disablement, or critical illness prevents you
from performing your job duties for at least one month. Payments stop when you can return to work or after the
maximum months opted, and overdue payments prior to the accident are excluded, subject to policy terms.

9. CLAIMS PROCEDURE (Refer “E. II. 35. Claims Notification and Procedure” of Policy Wordings)
CLAIMS NOTIFICATION AND PROCEDURE
If the Insured Person meets any accidental injury or suffers from Critical illness or any specific condition
covered under the Policy that may result in a claim under this policy, it is a condition precedent to Our liability
under the Policy that below procedure should be followed depending on the type of claim:
1. Cashless Claim Process (Applicable Only for “Section 11. Accidental Hospitalization Cover”):
Cashless Facility can be availed from our network hospitals only. This is facilitated by our Service Provider / Third
Party Administrator (TPA) and we would make a direct payment to the Network Hospital to the extent of Our
Liability provided that:
1. We are given a notice within 24 Hours of hospitalization in case of an emergency situation
2. For Cashless Facility You shall follow the below Procedure:
a. Share the Health Card/Copy of E-Cards along with ID Proof with the Hospital Authority & Obtain
the Pre-Authorization Form from the Hospital.
b. Submit Duly filled & Signed Pre-Authorization Form to the Hospital Counter.
c. Ensure that the Hospital shares the Duly filled & Signed Pre-Authorization Form to Service Provider
/ Third Party Administrator (TPA) for further Processing.
d. Service Provider / Third Party Administrator (TPA) will inform the decision and may issue
authorization letter depending on the Policy Terms and Conditions to the Hospital directly.
e. Once the request for Pre-Authorization has been granted, the treatment must take place within 15
days of the Pre-Authorization Approval Date or the Policy Expiry Date whichever is earlier and shall
be valid only if all the details of the Authorised details, Hospital and Location including Dates match
with the details of the Actual Treatment Received.
f. We reserve the right to modify, add or restrict any Network Provider for Cashless Facility in Our
sole discretion. Before availing Cashless Facility, please check the applicable updated list of Network
Providers.
g. For any queries designated Service Provider / Third Party Administrator (TPA) may be contacted on
the contact details mentioned on the Health Card/Copy of E-Cards issued to You.

12. THINGS TO REMEMBER
• Free Look Period- You may cancel the insurance policy if you do not want it, within 30 days from the beginning
of the policy. Please refer policy wordings for process of free look cancellation.
• Policy Renewal- Except on grounds of fraud, moral hazard or misrepresentation or non-cooperation, renewal
of your policy shall not be denied, provided the policy is not withdrawn.
• Migration & Portability- When your policy is due for renewal, you may migrate to another policy with us or
port your policy to another insurer. Please refer details of Migration and Portability policy as mentioned in the
Policy Wordings.
• Change in Sum Insured: Sum Insured can be changed (increased/decreased) only at the time of renewal or at
any time, subject to underwriting by the company. For increase in SI, the waiting period if any shall start afresh
only for the enhanced portion of the sum insured.
• Moratorium Period: After completion of sixty continuous months (five years) under the policy no look back to
be applied. This period of sixty months is called as moratorium period. The moratorium would be applicable
for the sums insured of the first policy and subsequently completion of five continuous years would be
applicable from date of enhancement of sums insured only on the enhanced limits. After the expiry of
Moratorium Period no health insurance policy shall be contestable except for proven fraud and permanent
exclusions specified in the policy contract.
