established fraud. The period of sixty continuous months is called as moratorium period. The moratorium will be applicable for the
sums insured of the first policy. Wherever, the sum insured is enhanced, completion of sixty continuous months would applicable
from the date of enhancement of sums insured only on the enhanced limits.
