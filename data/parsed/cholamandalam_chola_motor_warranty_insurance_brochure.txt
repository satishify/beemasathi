Chola Motor Warranty Insurance
(For used Cars excluding electric vehicles)
INTRODUCTION
Warranty is a tool by which a manufacturer of Motor Vehicles enhance the value of the product they
manufacture and sell.
Under `Chola Motor Warranty Insurance’, we offer protection to your vehicle components against repair or
replacement caused by breakdown or failure resulting from manufacturing defects. The Insurance can be
availed by customer at any time after the expiry of the original warranty given by the Manufacturer.
1. Who is providing coverage under the Policy?
Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company Ltd., a joint venture between Murugappa Group &
Mitsui Sumitomo Insurance Group, Japan is providing the coverage under the Policy.
2. Who is eligible for the coverage?
Any resident of India who owns a Motor Vehicle as defined in Central Motor Vehicles Act is eligible for
the coverage. Any Vehicle that is owned by a company, a partnership firm or a similar body corporate is
also eligible for the coverage.
3. What are the policy features?
a) The customer can buy `Chola Motor Warranty Insurance’ for used Cars both Private and commercial
(but excluding Electric vehicles) that are registered with transport Authorities in their name.
b) The coverage is available for repair or replacement of insured vehicle’s components due to
breakdown or failure resulting from manufacturing defect.
c) The Vehicle should be greater than 2 years of age from the date of first registration with Transport
Authorities. The maximum vehicle age limit for coverage under this policy is 10 years from the date
of first registration with Transport Authorities.
d) The policy covers Vehicle Components that are the parts of Aggregates or which make up the
aggregate and are listed below:
Components of each Aggregate are listed below which are forming part of the insured vehicle:-

road wheels. The chain of reactions which achieve that objective is set in motion by a spark, which
ignites a mixture of petrol vapour and compressed air inside a momentarily sealed cylinder and causes
it to burn rapidly.

2. Gear Box or Transmission system

torque. In a car, a transmission or a gearbox, acts as the medium that transmits power generated by
the engine to the wheels via a mechanical system of gears and gear trains. A transmission allows a
person to apply power to a vehicle in a controlled manner, without which, the car would not efficiently
move.

3. Differential Assembly
A differential is part of the front and/or rear axle assembly. The axle is the central shaft that the
wheels on the vehicle rotate around. The differential allows wheels on the same axle to rotate at
different speeds.
Components of Differential Assembly: Differential side gear or sun gears (Differential case, Pinion

4. Is the cover available on worldwide basis?
The cover is available for breakdown/failures of vehicle components happening only in India.
5. What is the Period of Insurance under the policy?
The policy will be issued for a period of one year and will commence from policy start date and hour as
specified in Schedule of the policy and will terminate either on utilization of 15,000 Kilometers or at
midnight on the expiry date as specified in the Policy Schedule whichever is earlier.

7. What will happen on Transfer of Ownership of the vehicle?
On the sale of the vehicle during the policy period, the `Chola Motor Warranty Insurance’ Policy will be
transferred to the subsequent owner for the remaining period and subject to the terms, conditions and
exclusions as specified in the Policy. The new insured should pay premium from the date of transfer till
the date of expiry of the policy on Company’s short period scales. The premium from the date of transfer
of the policy to the date of expiry will be refunded on pro-rata basis to the existing insured subject to no
admissible claim under the policy. On the date of transfer if it is found from the odometer reading that
15000 kilometers had already been utilized from the date of inception, then the policy will be cancelled
immediately and there will be no refund of premium and no transfer of policy.
