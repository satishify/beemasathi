Covers) limits specified in this Policy and the Policy Schedule, Insured Person

Accidental Injury.

consultations with qualified dieticians/nutritionists via video,
audio, or chat through our service provider network.

wellbeing consultations with emotional health
coaches/psychologists via video, audio, or chat through the our
service provider network.

12 Things to Free Look Cancellation: Insured has an option of cancelling his/her Section E

Policy Renewal: Except on grounds of fraud, moral hazard or mis
representation or non-co-operation, renewal of your policy shall not
be denied.
Migration and Portability: At renewal Insured has an option to
migrate his / her policy to other policy with us or port the policy to
another insurer subject to terms and conditions specified under
Migration and Portability guidelines.
For detailed guidelines on Migration and Portability, kindly refer the
link https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=393128
beneficiary will have the option to port the policy to other insurers by
applying to such insurer to port the entire policy along with all the

related to portability. If such person is presently covered and has been
continuously covered without any lapses under any health insurance

beneficiary will get the accrued continuity benefits in waiting periods

proposal for portability even if the policyholder has approached within
15 days from the renewal date of the existing policy, but in all such
cases acquiring insurer shall ensure that there is no break in policy.
Change in Sum Insured: sum insured can be changed (increased/
decreased) only at the time of renewal subject to underwriting by the
company. For increase in Sum insured, the waiting periods if any shall
start afresh only for the enhance portion of the sum insured.
Moratorium period: After the expiry of Moratorium Period no health
insurance policy shall be contestable except for proven fraud and
permanent exclusions specified in the policy contract. The
moratorium would be applicable for the sum insured of the first policy
and subsequently completion of 60 continuous months would be
applicable from date of enhancement of sums insured only on the
