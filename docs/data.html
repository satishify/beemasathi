Composition matters. Brochures and summaries are the majority of the corpus, so default similarity search tends to surface them. The target design treats document type as a hard authority signal for coverage questions, not a tiebreaker. The remaining documents are 26 endorsements and 2 IRDAI regulations; endorsements outrank summaries because they amend the contract.

Governance rules

  • Only insurer-owned or regulator domains are accepted; third-party mirrors are rejected.
  • PDFs are validated by signature and size; revision is resolved by date and revision marker.
  • Each document carries a sidecar with insurer, product, document type, source URL and UIN.
  • Each chunk carries source_rel, chunk_id, page_start/page_end, section_path, element_type, document_type, line_of_business, token_estimate and injection_flags under schema v2.
  • A document without provenance cannot enter the collection.
  • Each sync records SHA-256, chunk count and indexing time in the ingest ledger; re-ingesting a source replaces its chunks instead of duplicating them.

Rebuilding the corpus

The PDFs are third-party material and are not redistributed in the repository. The pipeline output — parsed text, chunk metadata and the ingest ledger — is committed so the corpus can be inspected and re-scored. 

./venv/bin/python scrapers/hybrid_scraper.py --dry-run   # preview discovery
./venv/bin/python scrapers/hybrid_scraper.py             # download official PDFs
./appctl.sh index                                        # parse, chunk, embed

Some insurer sites block automated collection. The scraper prints a manual fallback guide (--manual-guide) for those cases. A rebuild reproduces the pipeline, not byte-identical contents.

Known extraction limits

  • Page-sliced chunking cuts some clauses at page boundaries.
  • Table-aware extraction is available behind BEEMASATHI_TABLE_EXTRACT=1 and 39 table chunks are indexed, but many tables still arrive as flat text, so waiting-period and sub-limit data can be incomplete.
  • Six image-only PDFs fail extraction on every run; OCR fallback is not implemented yet.
  • 57% of chunks carry only a page label, so section-level citation is often approximate.
