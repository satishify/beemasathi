We issue this insurance policy to You and/or Your Family based on the information provided
by You/Proposer in the proposal form and premium paid by You/Proposer. This insurance is

Your/Insured Person/Insured/Policyholder/Proposer in this document refers to You and all the
Insured persons covered under this policy. The term Insurer/Us/Our/Company in this document
refers to Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company Limited.
