Product Introduction
At Digit, we understand the potential risk and liability associated with the ownership of a Private Car and
hence, we have designed “Digit Private Car Liability Only Policy” that offers a complete protection for:
• Liability to Third Parties
• Personal Accident Cover for Owner-Driver
What are the perils/liabilities for which cover is provided?
“Liability to Third Parties” provides protection for any legal liability arising out of the use of the vehicle for:
1. Death of or bodily injury to any person so far as it is necessary to meet the requirements of Motor Vehicles
Act
2. Damage to property other than property belonging to the insured or held in trust or in the custody or
control of the insured up to the limit specified in the schedule.
“Personal Accident Cover for Owner-Driver” provides Personal Accident cover for registered owner of the
vehicle who is the insured named in this policy holding an effective driving license at the time of the
accident.
