Prospectus
Chola Super Topup Insurance is an aggregate Topup plan that provides coverage for higher medical
expenses for families on a floating Sum Insured basis or on Individual Sum Insured basis.
1. Suitability
This Insurance is aimed at customers who have capacity to meet the Medical Expenses related to claims of
smaller size without insurance and are looking for insurance cover specifically to take care of large claims.
Customers who already have an Insurance Policy for certain limit either as individual policies or under their
corporate group policies but are looking for additional protection can avail Super Topup Insurance.
This Insurance will reimburse you upto the Sum insured in excess of deductible either on Individual or Floater
Sum Insured basis in respect of medically necessary costs as detailed in the policy.
2. Applicability
• Cover under this policy is available as
1. Individual Cover - Individual Sum insured for each members covered under the policy. Coverage
of Self/Proposer is not mandatory under Individual Cover.
2. Family Floater Cover– Single Sum Insured floats among the family members covered under the
policy. Coverage of Self/Proposer is mandatory under Family Floater Cover.
3. Age Limit
• Any person aged between 3 months and 70 years of age can avail the Insurance.
• Proposer should be 18 years of age (completed age).
4. Coverage Types
• You can cover all your family members (Self, Spouse, Children, Parents and other family members
like Parents in Laws, Grand Father, Grand Mother, Grand Son, Grand Daughter, Daughter in Law,
Son in Law, Sister, Brother in Law, Brother, Sister in Law, Nephew, Niece) on Individual Sum
Insured Basis. Each covered person will have an independent Sum Insured limit within the same
policy.
• You can cover yourself, spouse, Children and Parents upto a maximum of 6 members of your
family on floater Sum Insured basis. The Floater Sum insured will float over all the covered family
members.
• The premium paid for family members other than self, spouse, Dependent children and parents are
not eligible for IT exemption under 80D.
• This policy can be renewed lifelong subject to payment of renewal premium and Policy Terms and
conditions.
5. Policy Tenure
This policy can be availed for a policy term of 1 Year / 2 Years / 3 Years.

the Schedule will apply over aggregate of all admissible claims under the policy per annum by insured
(Individual cover) or insured family (in case of Family Floater cover).

for the amounts disallowed under any other policy/policies even if the sum insured is not exhausted.

policy.
iii. If the amount to be claimed exceeds the sum insured under a single policy, the Primary Insurer
shall seek the details of other available policies of the policyholder and shall coordinate with other
Insurers to ensure settlement of the balance amount as per the policy conditions.

indemnity basis, the insured person shall only indemnified the treatment costs in accordance with
the terms and conditions of the chosen policy.
