NRInsure
Add-On for My Health Care Plan
Policy Wordings
SECTION A) PREAMBLE
Whereas the Insured/ proposer has made a Proposal to Bajaj General Insurance Limited (Formerly known as Bajaj
Allianz General Insurance Company Limited) (hereinafter referred to as the “Company”) which is hereby agreed
to be the basis of this NRInsure Add-on (“Add-on”), and in consideration of payment of Premium, as specified in
the Schedule, the Company agrees, subject to the terms, conditions, limitations, exclusions, and definitions, to
indemnify Insured Person(s) or make payment, in excess of the amount of the Deductible and subject to the Sum
Insured and/or Limit of Indemnity, as is provided under this Rider.
SECTION B) S STANDARD AND SPECIFIC DEFINITIONS
All DEFINITIONS as defined in “SECTION B: DEFINITIONS” in the opted Base Policy Wordings are applicable mutatis
mutandis, to this Rider, unless otherwise stated herein.
Insured/ Insured Person for this add-on means Non-Resident Indians/ Overseas citizens of India named in the
Schedule.
Non-Resident Indian or NRI means an Indian citizen who is residing outside India and qualifies as a non-resident
under applicable Indian laws.
Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) Cardholders are foreign nationals registered in accordance with the provisions
laid down by the Ministry of Home Affairs and as stipulated under Section 7A of The Citizenship Act, 1955 (57 of
1955) and any subsequent amendment thereto.
SECTION C) OPERATIVE PARTS
Who can opt for the Add-on?
This Add-on may be opted by Insured Persons who are covered under the Company’s “My Health Care Plan”
Policy (“Base Policy”). This Add-on can only be purchased along with the Base Policy and cannot be opted for
independently.
OPERATIVE PARTS
If this Add-on is opted, Insured Person is entitled for a discount of 35% discount of the premium payable under
the base policy for Insured Person(s), subject to compliance with the conditions listed below.
i. The insured shall submit a declaration confirming that they are Non-Resident Indians / Overseas citizens of
India based abroad in entirety for the Policy Year.
ii. The Insured shall provide acceptable proof of overseas residence prior to or at the inception of Add-on and
at each subsequent renewal to continue availing the discount.
iii. The following documents must be submitted for verification at the time of inception and all subsequent
renewals:
• A copy of valid Passport with applicable Visa Stamping.
• Applicable Tax Documentation, Work Visa, or Employment/Work Permit outside of India.
• A signed Residential Agreement (lease/rental or ownership) in the overseas address.
• Any other document as may be specified by the Company from time to time.
iv. The Insured Person shall have and maintain an Indian bank account for payment of premium and for
facilitating claim payments, if any. The Company may seek proof of the same at inception or renewal.
v. All waiting periods, as per the Base Policy terms and conditions, shall continue to apply to the Insured Person
covered under this Add-on. This Add-on does not reduce, modify, or waive any waiting periods.
vi. If the Insured Person ceases to reside outside India, then the discount under this Add-on shall cease
immediately from next renewal. Premium shall thereafter be charged as per the applicable rate for residents
in India. The Insured Person(s) shall promptly intimate the Company of any change in the residency status.
vii. International Cover – Emergency Care only benefit under the Base Policy shall not be available to Insured
Person(s) availing this Add-on or to Non-Resident Indian/ Overseas citizens of India.

SECTION D) EXCLUSIONS UNDER THE ADD-ON – STANDARD AND SPECIFIC EXCLUSIONS
All exclusions applicable in Base Policy shall be applicable to this Add-on, mutatis mutandis.
Specific Exclusion
The Company shall not be liable to pay any Pre-Hospitalization and Post Hospitalisation Medical expenses
incurred outside India.
SECTION E) GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS – STANDARD AND SPECIFIC
All Terms and Conditions and General conditions of Base Policy read with Base Policy Schedule are applicable
mutatis mutandis, to this Add-on.
1. Opting Add-on-
a. This Add-on cannot be opted during mid-term of Base Policy.
b. Discount applicable, if any, on Base Policy will not be applicable on this Add-on.
2. Withdrawal of Add-on:
a. If opted Base Policy is withdrawn, then this Add-on shall also stand withdrawn.
b. ln the likelihood of this Add-on being withdrawn in future, the Company will intimate the Insured about
the same 90 days prior to expiry of the Base Policy.
3. Cancellation of Add-on: All the terms and conditions applicable to Cancellation of Base Policy shall mutatis
mutandis apply to the Cancelation of this Add-on.
4. Renewal of Add-on: In case of renewal of Base Policy, this opted Add-on shall be renewed provided
submission of relevant documents as stated in operative clause and Company’s receipt of prescribed
premium. Once Add-on is opted by Insured, Company will renew Base Policy together with Add-on and shall
not renew only Base Policy in isolation.
