Sum Insured, Insured’s Declared Value (IDV):
The Insured's Declared Value (IDV) of the vehicle will be deemed to be the ‘SUM INSURED’ for the purpose of
this policy which is fixed for each year of the policy term at the commencement of the contract for the insured
vehicle and shown on the policy schedule.
The IDV of the vehicle (and side car/accessories, if any, fitted to the vehicle) is to be fixed on the basis of the
manufacturer’s listed selling price of the brand and model as the insured vehicle at the commencement of
insurance/renewal and adjusted for depreciation (as per schedule specified below).
The schedule of age wise depreciation is applicable for the purpose of Total loss / Constructive Total Loss (TL /
CTL) claims only.
