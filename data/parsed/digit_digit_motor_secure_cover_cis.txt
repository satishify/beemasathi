SECTION – 1: MOTOR EXTENDED WARRANTY
If you have opted for this section, we will indemnify You against repair or replacement costs in respect of Your
Vehicle caused due to mechanical, electronic or electrical breakdown arising out of manufacturing defect
occurring during the Policy Period/ Risk period as mentioned in the Policy Schedule/ Certificate of Insurance.
Provided that the liability of the Company in respect of the Vehicle during the Policy Period shall not individually
or in aggregate exceed the Sum Insured as stated in the Policy Schedule/ Certificate of Insurance against this
section, subject to the terms, conditions, warranties and exclusions contained herein or endorsed or otherwise
expressed.
Provided that the mechanical, electronic or electrical breakdown should result in inability or incapacity of the
Vehicle to perform as per Manufacturer’s specifications under normal operating circumstances. Any breakdown
or gradual decline in vehicle’s performance due to age or usage shall not be considered an Insured Event under
this Section.
This section has two plans:
• Plan A: This plan shall be available only for First-hand vehicles where manufacturer’s warranty period is in
force. In such case, the cover shall start after the expiry of the Manufacturer Warranty Period, as defined in
this Policy.
• Plan B: This plan shall be available for vehicles where manufacturer warranty has lapsed as well as Pre-Owned
Vehicles. The cover shall commence from the date of purchase of the Vehicle/Equipment or as per Policy Tenure
defined in the Policy Schedule/Certificate of Insurance.

SECTION – 23: VEHICLE CYBER RISK COVER
If You have opted for this section, we will indemnify You, in excess of the amount of deductible if the following
loss(es) incurred by You as a consequence of a Cyber Attack on Your Vehicle including but not limited to
Hacking/ransomware/unauthorized access protection. Maximum upto the limit of sum insured as mentioned in the
Policy Schedule/Certificate of Insurance.
Cyber-related incidents shall include events arising from unauthorised access, unlawful control, data breaches,
malicious interference, software malfunction, cyber extortion, system vulnerability, or faulty software or firmware
updates, resulting in loss, damage, or liability, subject to the terms, conditions, and exclusions of this cover.
Coverage provided under Vehicle Cyber Risk Cover
1. Protection Against Unauthorised Access and Control – If someone illegally hacks into your vehicle’s software
driven control systems which results in vehicle to malfunction or accidents, this cover helps pay for the resulting
financial losses or repair costs.
2. Data Breach and Privacy Liability – Modern vehicles store and share data such as location, usage patterns, and
personal details. If a cyber attack leads to this data being stolen or misused, and you are legally held responsible,
this cover helps pay for legal claims made by affected persons and defense and related legal costs
3. Software and Firmware Update Malfunction - Vehicles often receive software updates over the air (OTA).
If a software or firmware update fails, gets corrupted, or introduces problems that causes your vehicle to stop
working properly, this cover helps pay for the resulting loss or damage.
4. Cyber Extortion and Ransomware – If cyber criminals lock, disable, or threaten your vehicle’s electronic or
software systems and demand extortion money, this cover helps with financial assistance and expert support in
the event of such cyber extortion demands.
5. Physical Damage Resulting from Cyber Incidents – Indemnity for repairs of physical damage to the insured
vehicle directly resulting from a cyber- attack.
6. Third-Party Liability - If a cyber incident involving your vehicle causes harm to others in ways not covered by
standard Motor Third Party insurance, this cover helps protect you against legal claims arising from such cyber
related liabilities.
