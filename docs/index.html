BeemaSathi is a retrieval-augmented copilot for Indian health and motor insurance. It reads official insurer and IRDAI documents, cites the clause and page behind every coverage claim, separates binding wordings from marketing brochures, and refuses when a product is not loaded instead of guessing.

Why this exists

Indian retail insurance is sold as a short brochure and governed by a long policy wording. The facts a customer needs — waiting periods, room-rent caps, sub-limits, co-pay, pre-existing disease conditions, exclusions, cashless rules — sit in 30–100 page PDFs that change by insurer, product, UIN and revision year. The brochure summarises the product; it is not the contract.

  • A room-rent choice above the cap proportionally reduces every other claim head, not just the room charge.
  • Treatment inside a pre-existing disease waiting period is rejected even when the hospitalisation itself is covered.
  • Delayed intimation or a non-network hospital turns a cashless claim into reimbursement with a different document set.
  • A customer reading a brochure can reasonably conclude a benefit exists that the wording excludes.

General-purpose chatbots make this worse: they answer confidently from generic insurance knowledge, cannot distinguish a brochure from a binding wording, and cannot say “this product is not in my knowledge base”.

What the system does

Retrieves from primary sources

Hybrid dense and BM25 retrieval over official insurer documents and IRDAI regulations, with insurer, product and document-type filters.

Ranks authority, not prose

A policy wording outranks a CIS, prospectus or brochure. Summary documents are labelled as summaries in the answer.

Refuses when evidence is missing

An unloaded insurer or product produces a named gap and a refusal, never an answer borrowed from another insurer.

Shows its sources

Every answer carries the insurer, product, section, page and document type used as evidence.

Checks claim-rejection risk

Rule-based assessment of network status, room-rent cap, PED waiting period, disclosure and intimation timing.

Blocks unsafe advice

Hiding a pre-existing disease, backdating admission or fabricating bills is refused by the guardrails.

Run it locally

The application is a Streamlit client over a FastAPI service. It answers with a hosted Qwen3-8B endpoint or a local Ollama model.

git clone https://github.com/satishify/beemasathi.git
cd beemasathi
python3 -m venv venv
./venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt

cp .env.example .env        # add the model API base URL, key and CA cert
./appctl.sh start           # http://127.0.0.1:8501

Without .env the app falls back to local Ollama (ollama pull qwen2.5:7b). The vector index is built from the corpus with ./appctl.sh index.
