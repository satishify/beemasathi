12 Things to Free Look Cancellation: Policy holder/Insured member has an option of
remember cancelling his/her policy up to 30 days from the first inception of policy with
Us, subject to rest terms and conditions.
Policy Renewal: Except on grounds of fraud, moral hazard or mis
representation or non-co-operation, renewal of your policy shall not be
denied.
Migration and Portability: At renewal Insured has an option to migrate his
/her policy to other policy with us or port the policy to another insurer subject
to terms and conditions specified under Migration and Portability guidelines
For detailed guidelines on Migration and Portability, kindly refer the link
https://irdai.gov.in/document-detail?documentId=393128

Moratorium period: After the expiry of Moratorium Period no health
insurance policy shall be contestable except for proven fraud and permanent
exclusions specified in the policy contract.
