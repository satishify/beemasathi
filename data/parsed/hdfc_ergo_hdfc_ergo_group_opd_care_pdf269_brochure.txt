Migration and Portability: When your policy is due for renewal, you may migrate to another policy with As per base product

Process for migration: The Insured Person will have the option to migrate the Policy to other health
insurance products/plans offered by the Company by applying for Migration of the policy atleast 30 days As per base product
before the policy renewal date as per IRDAI guidelines on Migration.
Process for portability: The Insured Person will have the option to port the Policy to other insurers by
applying to such Insurer to port the entire policy along with all the members of the family, if any, at least As per base product
30 days before, but not earlier than 60 days from the policy renewal date as per IRDAI guidelines related
to Portability.
