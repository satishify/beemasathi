i. In case of multiple policies taken by an insured person during a period from one or more insurers to

the insurer shall independently settle the claim subject to the terms and conditions of this policy.
iii. If the amount to be claimed exceeds the sum insured under a single policy, the Primary Insurer shall
seek the details of other available policies of the policyholder and shall coordinate with other Insurers
to ensure settlement of the balance amount as per the policy conditions.
iv. Where an insured person has policies from more than one insurer to cover the same risk on indemnity
basis, the insured person shall only indemnified the treatment costs in accordance with the terms and
