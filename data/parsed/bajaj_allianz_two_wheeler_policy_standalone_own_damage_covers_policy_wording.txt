i arranging for the assistance of a vehicle technician to replace the flat tyre with the spare stepney tyre of

carrying out the repairs, would be borne by You.

cities as listed in the attached annexure and the Insured Vehicle has not reached a workshop/ repairer.

breakdown, We will arrange for minor mechanical and/or electrical repairs to make the Insured Vehicle mobile
again provided the breakdown has occurred within 100 kilometres from the center of the cities as listed in
the attached annexure and the Insured Vehicle has not reached a workshop/ repairer.

breakdown, We will send urgent message on Your request to the specified persons through available means
of communication.

immobilization and / or towing of the Insured Vehicle to Our nearest preferred workshop, provided the event
has occurred within 100 kilometres from the center of the cities as listed in the attached annexure and the
Insured Vehicle has not reached a workshop/repairer.
8. Towing Facility: In the event of the Insured Vehicle getting immobilized as a result of Accident and/or
breakdown, We shall arrange for towing away of the Insured Vehicle from the spot of immobilization to our
nearest preferred workshop/ repairer provided the Accident/ breakdown has occurred within 100 kilometres
from the center of the cities as listed in the attached annexure and the Insured Vehicle has not reached a
workshop/repairer.
9. Taxi Benefits: In the event of the Insured Vehicle meeting with an Accident /breakdown, We will arrange for
a free travel of the occupants of the Insured Vehicle to a single destination within a vicinity of 40 kilometres
from the spot of immobilization through a taxi or any other transportation service provided the event has
occurred within 100 kilometres from the center of the cities as listed in the attached annexure and the Insured
Vehicle has to be towed away to a workshop/ repairer.
