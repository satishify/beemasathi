HDFC ERGO General Insurance Company Limited
Policy Wording
Cumulative Bonus Plus (Group)
A. Preamble
Cumulative Bonus Plus (Group) is an add-on that can only be opted and shall only function along with
an active HDFC ERGO Group health Base Product. This add-on cannot be opted in isolation or as a
separate product.
B. Operative Clause
a. This Add-on can be opted without any additional premium only at inception or at the time
renewal of Base Policy.
b. All other general terms & conditions, exclusions, clauses, eligibility rules, and definitions
applicable to the Base Product will apply to this add-on unless specifically stated otherwise in
this document or on the Policy Schedule of the Base Policy.
c. Policy duration of this Add-on shall be same as that of the Base Policy.
d. Base Policy or Base Product means the Group health product to which this add-on is
attached
C. Coverage
Coverage provided under any type of cumulative bonus cover, which increases the coverage amount,
post completion of a Policy Year, of the base policy, shall be superseded to the extent of coverage as
detailed hereunder.
a. The applicable bonus under the base policy shall be provided irrespective of any claims made
during a Policy Year.
b. Clause in the Base Policy pertaining to reduction in bonus post completion of policy year owing
to a claim during the Policy Year shall stand void.
c. All other terms and conditions applicable to the benefit providing cumulative bonus under the
base policy shall remain same.
