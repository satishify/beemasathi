• Details of procedure to be followed for Cashless service as well as for reimbursement of claim
1. Intimation of claim to TATA AIG through various mediums available (Website –
www.tataaig.com, TATA AIG App, WhatsApp - Send “Hi” on “9136160375”, TATA AIG Customer
App on Google play & Apple store).
2. First contact team connects with Insured to confirm workshop location and confirm if the
vehicle is placed for repairs.
3. Appointment of Surveyor by TATA AIG.
4. Documents are verified by the Surveyor, and if all documents are in order, repair approval is
shared immediately.
5. Once the repair works are completed by the workshop, re-inspection of the vehicle may be
carried out, if required.
6. Upon submission of repair invoice to TATA AIG in case of Cashless Claims, delivery order is
shared with workshop, post which Insured can take delivery of vehicle.
7. In case of reimbursement claims, Insured will have to submit repair invoice in original and a
copy of cancelled cheque to TATA AIG.
8. Claim payment is done through NEFT within 7 days from receipt of all relevant documents.
