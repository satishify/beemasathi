ROAD AMBULANCE ADD-ON
Add-On for “Group Guard” and “Flexi Guard (Group)”
Policy Wordings
SECTION A) PREAMBLE (Introduction to the Policy)
The Insured, as described in the Base Policy, has submitted an additional proposal to Bajaj General Insurance
Company with Undertakings, declarations, information and consent or which are mentioned in a Transcript
which forms the basis of an Insurance contract.
It is further understood that the Insured holding the insurance policy has agreed to such additional terms
with the Company, which includes paying an additional premium. In return, the insurance company will cover
certain expenses incurred during the Add-on period, as long as these are within the terms of the Add-on
coverage.
SECTION B) DEFINITIONS (Meaning of the Term)
All DEFINITIONS as defined in “SECTION B: DEFINITIONS” in the opted Base Policy Wordings are applicable
mutatis mutandis, to this Add-on.
Base Policy – For the purpose of this Add-On, Base policy is “Group Guard” Insurance policy and Group
Guard Section of “Flexi Guard (Group)”
SECTION C) Road Ambulance Cover (Benefit for which YOU are Covered)
Who can opt for this Add-On?
Insured who have opted for “Group Guard” and Group Guard Section of “Flexi Guard (Group)” Insurance
Policy and (“Base Policy”) of the Company can purchase/opt for this Add-on for Insured Beneficiaries who are
covered under the Base Policy.
Operative Parts – (Simplifying the Coverage)
We will indemnify You against the Reasonable and Customary Expenses incurred by You on Road ambulance
services provided by a healthcare or ambulance service provider for:
• Transferring You to the nearest Hospital having adequate emergency facilities for the provision of
health services following an Emergency or.
• Transferring You from the Hospital where You were admitted initially to another Hospital with higher
medical facilities.
Claim under this section shall be payable only:
i. If We have accepted Your Claim under Accidental Hospitalization Expenses section of Base Policy"
ii. Up to the actual expenses subject to maximum of Sum Insured Limit as specified in Certificate of
Insurance for this cover.
SECTION D) EXCLUSIONS (What is Not covered)
All exclusions applicable in opted Base Policy shall be applicable to this Add-on, with any adjustments as may
be applicable.
SECTION E) GENERAL CONDITIONS
All Policy Terms and Conditions and General conditions of opted Base Policy read with Policy Schedule are
applicable to this Add-on, with any adjustments as may be applicable.
1. Opting Add on-
i. This Add-on cannot be opted during mid-term of Base Policy.
ii. Once opted, the Add-on will become an integral part of the Base Policy.
2. Cancellation of Add on-
All the terms and conditions as to Cancellation of Base Policy shall mutatis mutandis apply to the
Cancelation of Add-on.

3. Renewal of Add on-
In case of renewal of opted Base Policy, opted Add-on shall be automatically renewed subject to
Company’s receipt of prescribed premium. Once Add-on is opted by Insured Beneficiary(ies), We will
renew Base Policy together with Add-on and shall not renew only Base Policy.
4. Withdrawal of Add on-
i. If opted Base Policy is withdrawn, then this Add-on shall also stand withdrawn.
ii. ln the likelihood of this product being withdrawn in future, We will intimate the Insured Beneficiary
about the same 90 days prior to expiry of the Base Policy.
iii. Insured Beneficiary will have the option to migrate to similar health insurance product available
with the Company at the time of renewal.
