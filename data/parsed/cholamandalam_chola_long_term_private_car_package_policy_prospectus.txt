PROSPECTUS
This prospectus is designed for our end customers who plan to or have already bought Chola Long Term Private Car
Package Policy for their cars from Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company. Details about policy coverages,
exclusions are part of this document.
Why do I need to insure my Vehicle?
Insuring your vehicle protects you from a financial loss arising out of loss or damage to your vehicle, your liability
towards third parties for personal injury, death and property damage on account of any accident involving your
vehicle. Furthermore, you are also responsible for the safety of yourself.
Under the provisions of Motor Vehicles Act, all the vehicles that ply in public places must have an insurance policy
that at least covers ‘Third Party Liability’ as specified under the act.
1. Who is providing coverage under the Policy?
Cholamandalam MS General Insurance Company Ltd., a joint venture between Murugappa Group & Mitsui
Sumitomo Insurance Group, Japan is providing the coverage under the Policy.
2. Who is eligible for the coverage?
Any resident of India who owns a Private car as defined in Central Motor Vehicles Act is eligible for the coverage.
Any Vehicle that is owned by a company, a partnership firm or a similar body corporate is also eligible for the
coverage.
3. What is the coverage under Chola long term Private Car package policy?
This is a comprehensive policy which covers both ‘Third Party liability (Act Only Cover)’ and ‘Own Damage Cover’
for up to 3 years.

Section II - Third Party (TP) Liability:
➢ Personal Liability: As per the latest provisions of Motor Vehicles Act.
➢ Property Liability: In the event of your vehicle causing ‘Third Party’ property damage, Chola MS will offer
compensation up to Rs 7, 50, 000.
Section III - Compulsory Personal Accident (CPA) cover for Owner-Driver: –
The CPA cover offers compensation upto Rs.15 lakhs for personal injuries suffered by the registered Owner of the
Insured Vehicle whilst driving, travelling or alighting into/out of the Insured Vehicle. The registered Owner should
hold an effective driving license. The Compensation will be paid to the nominee in an unfortunate event of death
of the registered vehicle owner.

4. What is the maximum age upto which the product offers cover?
Up to 15 years old vehicle.
5. What is the Sum Insured under the policy?
The Sum Insured which is known as Insured’s declared value (IDV) is the maximum amount which Chola MS is
liable at the time of a claim under the policy. IDV is the value arrived at the commencement of policy based on
the Manufacturer’s listed selling price of the brand and model of the vehicle insured less depreciation based on
the Age of the Vehicle.
The schedule of age-wise depreciation as shown below is applicable for the purpose of Total Loss/ Constructive
Total Loss (TL / CTL) claims only.
THE SCHEDULE OF DEPRECIATION FOR FIXING IDV OF THE VEHICLE
AGE OF THE VEHICLE % OF DEPRECIATION FOR FIXING IDV
Not exceeding 6 months 5%
Exceeding 6 months but not exceeding 1 year 15%
Exceeding 1 year but not exceeding 2 years 20%
Exceeding 2 years but not exceeding 3 years 30%
IDV shall be treated as the ‘Market Value’ throughout the policy period without any further depreciation for the
purpose of Total Loss (TL) / Constructive Total Loss (CTL) claims.
The insured vehicle shall be treated as a CTL if the aggregate cost of retrieval and / or repair of the vehicle,
subject to terms and conditions of the policy, exceeds 75% of the IDV of the vehicle.
6. Is the cover available on worldwide basis?
The cover is available for any loss or damage of vehicle insured happening only in India. However, on payment
of additional premium the policy can be extended to cover the following Countries by way of Endorsements:
a) Bangladesh b) Bhutan c) Nepal d) Pakistan e) Sri Lanka f) Maldives
7. What is the period of Insurance under the policy?
Section I – OD 2/3 years Section II – TP 2/3 years Section III – CPA 1/2/3 years
