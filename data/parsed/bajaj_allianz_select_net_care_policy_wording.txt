SELECTNET CARE RIDER
POLICY WORDINGS
SECTION A) PREAMBLE
Whereas the insured described in the Base Policy Schedule hereto (hereinafter called the ‘Insured’ or “Policyholder”
or “Insured Person” or “Proposer” or ‘You’) has made to Bajaj General Insurance Limited (Formerly known as Bajaj
Allianz General Insurance Co. Ltd.) (hereinafter called the “Company” or “insurance company” or “Insurer” or “Bajaj
General” or “We”), an additional proposal or an additional Proposal as mentioned in the transcript of the additional
Proposal, containing certain undertakings, declarations, information/particulars and statements, which shall be the
basis of this Rider insurance Contract [“Rider”] and is deemed to be incorporated herein, and has paid the additional
premium specified in the Rider as consideration for this Rider, now the Company agrees, subject always to the Base
Policy and the following terms, conditions, exclusions, and limitations (other than time bound exclusions) of the
Base Policy, and in excess of the amount of the Deductible, to additionally indemnify the Insured/ Insured Member,
as the case may be, in the manner and to the extent during the Rider Period provided hereinafter.
SECTION B) DEFINITIONS- STANDARD AND SPECIFIC DEFINITIONS
All DEFINITIONS as defined in “SECTION B: DEFINITIONS” in the opted Base Policy Wordings are applicable mutatis
mutandis, to this rider.
Preferred Provider Network (PPN) is a network of healthcare providers that offer services to policyholders at
reduced rates. These providers are contracted by insurance companies and may include doctors, hospitals, and
other medical facilities. PPNs are designed to provide cashless services to policyholders who fall under their
umbrella.
SECTION C) BENEFITS COVERED UNDER THE POLICY
In case this rider is opted, Insured person is entitled for a discount of 20% of the premium payable under the base
policy. The insured is eligible to avail treatment in PPN hospital as per list available on the digital platform of Insurer
or website, subject to following criteria:
i. The In-patient Hospitalization claim should be availed on cashless basis.
ii. Co-payment of 20% for each and every claim payable under the In-patient Hospitalization Treatment section
shall be applicable, in case
a. Hospitalization other than listed under the PPN Network.
b. Reimbursement claim availed.
Note- Kindly refer to the Company’s website for list of PPN hospitals.
I. Who can opt for this Rider?
Only Insured who have opted for the Company’s Health Policy (“Policy” or “Base Policy”) can purchase/opt for
this Rider for Insured himself and/or his/her family members [“Insured Member/s”].
II. Rider Period:
Rider Period shall be as per the tenure of the Base Policy viz. if Base Policy is for 1 year then the Rider Period shall
be for 1 year and if Base Policy is for 2 years then the Rider Period shall be for 2 years.
SECTION D) EXCLUSIONS UNDER THE RIDER – SPECIFIC EXCLUSIONS
All exclusions applicable in opted Base Policy shall be applicable to this Rider, mutatis mutandis.

SECTION E) SPECIFIC GENERAL CONDITIONS APPLICABLE TO THIS RIDER
All Policy Terms and Conditions and General conditions of opted Base Policy read with Policy Schedule are
applicable to this Rider mutatis mutandis.
1. Opting Rider:
This Rider cannot be opted during mid-term of Policy.
2. Withdrawal of Rider:
a. If Base Policy is withdrawn by Company, then this Rider shall also stand withdrawn automatically without any
separate notice/intimation.
b. In the likelihood of this product being withdrawn in future, the Company will intimate the Insured Person about
the same 90 days prior to expiry of the Base Policy.
3. Cancellation of Rider:
All the terms and conditions as to Cancellation of Base Policy shall mutatis mutandis apply to the Cancelation of
Rider.
4. Renewal of Rider:
In case of renewal of Opted Base Policy, opted Rider shall be automatically renewed subject to Company’s receipt
of prescribed premium. Once Rider is opted by Insured, Company will renew Base Policy together with Rider and
shall not renew only Base Policy.
