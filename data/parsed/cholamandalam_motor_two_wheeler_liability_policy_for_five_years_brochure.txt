Why do I need to insure my Vehicle?
Under the provisions of Motor Vehicles Act, all the vehicles that ply in public places must have an insurance policy
that at least covers ‘Third Party Liability’ as specified under the act.
What is the coverage under the policy?
Liability to Third Parties (TP) - Cover for Legal liability due to Accident resulting in
o Personal Injury of TP – Unlimited coverage as per Court Award for accidental Death or injury of the Third
party
o Property damage of TP – Upto Rs.1,00,000 for accidental damage to property of Third party
Compulsory Personal Accident (CPA) cover for Owner-Driver:– The CPA cover offers compensation upto
Rs.15 lakhs for personal injuries suffered by the registered Owner of the Insured Vehicle whilst driving or alighting
into/out of the Insured Vehicle. The registered Owner should hold an effective driving license. The Compensation
will be paid to the nominee in an unfortunate event of death of the registered vehicle owner.

Five years

Insurance Act 1938, Section 41 - Prohibition of Rebates
